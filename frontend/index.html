What it checks

Three content types, one scanner

Every format gets its own analysis method, tuned to the signals that actually differ between AI and human-made content.

Image scanning

Looks at noise texture, color distribution, and edge sharpness to spot the tell-tale smoothness of generated images.

Video scanning

Samples frames evenly across the timeline and runs each one through our image model, then combines the votes.

Text scanning

Measures sentence rhythm, vocabulary variety, and repetition patterns — the same signals linguists use to spot machine phrasing.

Process

How a scan happens

From upload to result, here's exactly what happens to your content.

1

You submit content

Drag and drop a file, or paste text directly into the detector page. Nothing is uploaded until you press Analyze.

2

The scanner runs

Our backend measures the relevant statistical signals (or calls an open AI model, if configured) and calculates a score.

3

You get a verdict

A clear label — Likely AI Generated or Likely Human Created — plus a confidence percentage and the raw signals behind it.
